What would help next time
Self-check before building a "with-the-feature" demo

When a demo's job is to show a mechanic (an interview, a calibration step, a confirmation), add one explicit check before building the comparison artifact: "did I actually run the real mechanic with the real user, or reuse a prior artifact?" The interview-skip miss was a one-line check away from not happening.

Corpus growth from real accepts, not synthetic authoring

The local judge's original bug came from a synthetic-authoring scheme mismatch. The next lever (growing from real label_finding accepts) avoids reintroducing the same class of labeling risk.
