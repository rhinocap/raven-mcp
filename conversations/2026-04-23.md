## This session

### v1.3.0 scope — three new top-level layers
Andrew asked Raven to learn:
1. **Research &amp; data analysis best practices** — how to measure success for an app (conversion, metrics, frameworks) and how to set up usability studies appropriate to what's built.
2. **Service design best practices** — how to analyze and coach someone on creating a quality service (signup, help moments, human handoff). Plus an HTML service-blueprint generator that can show *current* state and *ideal* state.
3. **Visual design &amp; branding trends** — logos, imagery, gradients, system thinking. Looking at current best-practice trends.

All sourced from publicly available knowledge: NN/g, Stickdorn, Shostack, GOV.UK, Polaine, Kahneman (peak-end), Intercom (RICE), Google (HEART), McClure (AARRR), Pentagram case studies, Brand New, and public brand systems at Stripe, Airbnb, Linear, Vercel.

### Files authored — research layer
- `src/data/research/principles/research-fundamentals.json` — 11 principles (match method to question, observe behavior not opinion, sample size matters, minimize bias, triangulate, separate observation from interpretation, start with the decision, context of use, recruit to target population, ethics, tie findings to action)
- `src/data/research/methods/qualitative.json` — interviews, contextual inquiry, diary studies, field studies, intercepts
- `src/data/research/methods/quantitative.json` — surveys, product analytics, A/B testing, task benchmarking, log/clickstream analysis
- `src/data/research/methods/usability.json` — moderated, unmoderated, 5-second test, card sort, tree test, heuristic eval
- `src/data/research/frameworks/metrics-frameworks.json` — HEART, AARRR, North Star, Conversion Funnel, RICE, OKRs (each with structure, when-to-use, pitfalls, examples)

### Files authored — service design layer
- `src/data/service-design/principles/service-design-principles.json` — 10 principles (service as experience over time, frontstage/backstage, Stickdorn's 5 — human-centered/sequential/evidencing/holistic, moments of truth, peak-end, handoff design, offering human help)
- `src/data/service-design/patterns/service-blueprinting.json` — 5-row structure, when to blueprint, current-vs-ideal, unhappy-path blueprinting

### Files authored — brand layer
- `src/data/brand/principles/logo-usage.json` — clear space, min size, distortion prohibitions, placement, restraint
- `src/data/brand/principles/gradient-usage.json` — serve hierarchy, limit palette/direction, contrast floor, trend-vs-signature
- `src/data/brand/principles/imagery.json` — consistency, representation, purpose (not decoration)
