**2026-07-29 changeover — the series is NOT continuous across this line.** From this date, design-judge Stop-hook catches are logged in their own row and are NOT deducted from first-attempt accuracy. Rationale (Fable 5 consult, 2026-07-29): a zero-cost net that catches ≥3 claims/session is the mechanism WORKING; logging each catch as an execution-discipline miss double-billed it, and four consecutive revisits re-litigated it as a behavior problem when a `Stop` hook is structurally incapable of firing before a claim. The prose demand to pre-run the gate was removed from `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md` in the same change. Pre-2026-07-29 accuracy figures still include these catches, so expect an apparent jump that reflects accounting, not behavior.

**Autonomy is now scored as a ~90% BAND, not a maximum** — below 90 and above 95 are equally off-target. 97-99% meant load-bearing questions were being suppressed and paid back as rework (see the 2026-07-21/24 coachmark entry: the four-field interview was run anyway, after the wasted build). Expect autonomy to FALL and first-attempt accuracy to rise; that is the intended trade.
