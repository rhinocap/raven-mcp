### Content design systems — new top-level category
**What:** Added `content/` as a fifth layer alongside principles, patterns, business, and tokens. Three sub-kinds mirroring the overall Raven taxonomy:
- **Brand content systems** (`src/data/content/systems/`) — 5 real-world voice & tone guides: Mailchimp, GOV.UK, Shopify Polaris, Atlassian, Intuit. Each JSON has voice attributes, tone shifts by context (onboarding/errors/billing/success), vocabulary (use/avoid/never), grammar rules, content patterns for errors+empty-states+buttons, and inclusive language guidance.
- **UX-writing principles** (`src/data/content/principles/ux-writing.json`) — 11 principles: clarity over cleverness, front-load meaning, active voice, be specific not generic, acknowledge the user, match words to mental model, consistent terminology, error-message anatomy, scannable structure, inclusive language, voice vs tone.
- **Content patterns** (`src/data/content/patterns/`) — 4 pattern files: error messages (inline validation, form submission failure, system-wide failure, permission denied), empty-state copy (first-use, no-results, completion, error-adjacent), notifications (activity, status, email, push, in-app toast), form validation (labels, placeholders, helper text, inline success, required marking).

**Wiring:**
- `src/index.ts` — added `CONTENT_DIR` + subdirs, loader functions (`loadContentRegistry`, `loadContentSystem`, `getAvailableContentSystemIds`), and merged content principles/patterns into the existing `allPrinciples`/`allPatterns` arrays so they're automatically reachable through `get_principles`, `get_pattern`, and `search_knowledge`.
- 4 new discoverable tools: `list_content_systems`, `get_content_system` (with optional `section` filter for voice/tone_shifts/vocabulary/grammar/content-patterns/inclusive-language), `get_content_principles` (filter by writing context), `get_content_pattern` (enum of 4 types).
- `extractInsight` extended so each new tool logs a small insight object (system id, section, context preview, type) to the local usage log — no raw content ever written.
- `manifest.json` updated: 4 new tool entries, description now says "content design systems", keywords add `content-design`, `ux-writing`, `voice-and-tone`.
- `README.md` updated: five layers, new tool rows in the table, data-structure block shows `content/`.
