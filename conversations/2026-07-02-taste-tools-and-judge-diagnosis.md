**Pattern worth naming (generalizable, queued to PROMOTION-QUEUE.md):** when auditing a data pipeline's categorical/verdict-mapping function against a corpus assembled from multiple schemes, don't trust the mapper's overall output counts — grep for every scheme actually present in the corpus's source files and confirm the mapper has an explicit branch for each. A silent default-return (`return "PASS"` for an unrecognized verdict) doesn't throw, doesn't show up in totals, and only surfaces as inexplicable downstream instability (checkpoints flip between under-flagging and over-flagging) — exactly what made this bug take a full `grade.py` sweep across 4 checkpoints to notice instead of a 30-second code read.
