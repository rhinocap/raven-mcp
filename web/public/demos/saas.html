What Flux measures

Each number traces back to a specific event in your repository: a commit, a review, a merge, a deploy. Click any figure and Flux shows the events underneath it.

01  /  Pulse metrics

The four DORA measures, computed from webhooks

Deploy frequency, lead time for changes, change failure rate, and time to restore. Flux derives all four from deploy and incident webhooks, so nobody has to keep a spreadsheet current on Friday afternoons.

02  /  Cycle time

Every pull request, timed across four stages

Coding, review, QA, deploy. Flux records the median hours a change spends in each one. The longest stage is where next week goes, and on most teams it is not the one people name.

  • Coding8.2h
  • Review11.4h
  • QA3.1h
  • Deploy0.9h

Review work concentrates quietly. Flux counts reviews completed per person alongside focus-time fragmentation and after-hours commit share, so load can be rebalanced while it is still a scheduling problem.
